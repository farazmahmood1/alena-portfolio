Who we are

Codilated is an AI-first Shopify brand-building and software agency working with brands worldwide, with a special focus on the UAE, the USA and Pakistan. We take e-commerce brands from idea to first sales — brand identity, custom Shopify stores and launch marketing — and build custom software, apps and AI automations that help them scale.

Selected work

Our story

We kept meeting the same founder. They had a great product, a beautiful website, and almost no sales. Their designer had handed over a logo, their developer had installed a theme, and their marketer was running ads to a store that didn't convert. Nobody owned the result. Codilated was built to fix that. Muhammad Hashim Aqeel started the agency in 2019 with one idea: a brand, its store and the systems behind it should be designed by one team that is accountable for sales — not just for deliverables. Today we are engineers, brand designers and growth specialists working as one unit. We've shipped 124+ projects and launched 30+ brands from scratch, from celebrity-led skincare stores to wellness catalogues to custom software and AI agents. The approach hasn't changed: clarity first, craft always, results that compound.

Mission and vision

Mission: To help founders build brands that sell — by combining sharp brand identity, conversion-focused Shopify stores and AI automation into one working system. Vision: To be the most trusted brand-and-technology partner for growing e-commerce brands worldwide — starting with the Gulf, North America and South Asia.

Built for operators

What we believe

A store without a system is a car without an engine. Looks matter, but the system behind the store is what turns visitors into revenue. Business case first. Every project is tied to revenue earned or cost saved before we design a pixel or write a line of code.

Craft is non-negotiable. Custom code over bloated plugins. Real brand systems over one-off logos. Automate the busywork: your team should sell, create and decide — not copy-paste. You own everything: brand files, code, accounts and data belong to you. No lock-in, ever.
