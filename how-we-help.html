Hello, we're Codilated and we work with companies, founders and scale-ups to get their products built, launched and sold in a way that keeps working.

→ STRATEGY WITH SUBSTANCE

Decide what to build before building it — and what not to build at all

We start every engagement by working out where your time and money actually go, then rank the possible changes by payback rather than by enthusiasm. What you get is not a strategy deck — it is a costed plan with agreed success metrics, and an honest view on which parts are not worth doing. Saying no to the wrong project is usually the highest-value hour of the whole engagement.

Growth services
Map where the time actually goes
A thorough audit of your workflows and funnel shows exactly where hours and money are being lost, so the plan targets the real constraint rather than the obvious one.
Rank by payback, not enthusiasm
A proper cost/benefit pass on each candidate lets us build the shortlist in order of return, and say plainly which items are not worth doing.
Architecture that stays cheap to change
Decisions made now decide what year two costs. We design for maintainability and clear boundaries rather than for the fastest possible first release.
A plan you can actually execute
A detailed, sequenced delivery plan turns the strategy into scheduled work with owners, dependencies and agreed success metrics.

→ BUILD THAT HOLDS UP

Software that survives year two — typed, tested and documented

We build in short increments you can review, against your real data rather than a demo fixture. AI agents, web platforms, storefronts and integrations all ship with tests, logging and monitoring, because the thing that determines whether a system is an asset or a liability is how cheap it is to change later. Everything is handed over documented, so staying with us is a choice rather than a dependency.

WHERE ARE YOU?

Which of these best describes where your business is right now?

01 → We are early

One foot on the start line — how do we get you to something real, fast, without building the wrong thing?

You have the product vision; we bring the engineering and the go-to-market. We will push hard on scope first, because early teams rarely fail from building too little. Expect a narrow, working first version in weeks rather than months, instrumented from day one so you learn something real from it.

Early-stage teams
Marketing Start-Ups

02 → We are growing

The wheel is turning and you are making progress — how do we keep it moving without the wheels coming off?

You have traction and now the constraints are operational: manual work eating the team, a codebase getting expensive to change, a funnel nobody can fully explain. This is where automation and proper architecture pay for themselves fastest. We find the specific bottleneck rather than proposing a rebuild.

Growing businesses
Marketing founders

03 → We are established

The whistle has gone and your team is on the pitch — how do we win it strategically rather than just play harder?

You have scale, systems and a team, and the question is where the next step change comes from. Usually it is AI automation on high-volume processes, predictive analytics on data you already hold, or replatforming something that has quietly become the constraint. We will tell you which, and what it is worth.
