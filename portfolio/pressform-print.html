In short

We worked with Pressform Print  on repositioning the business and rebuilding how it sells.

The first and most consequential decision was narrowing the offer to the three product lines that actually made money, so the site could sell them properly. Automating instant quotes for standard jobs while routing complex communication is what made the project genuinely profitable rather than merely modern. Combined with focused local campaigns we grew online orders substantially without cannibalising the custom work.

Design system

Instant quotes where possible, humans where it matters

The sharp black-and-white system gives the brand real presence, while the single accent red acts as the signature accent that marks every call to action across the store.

Credibility and character

A clear identity, local roots and no marketing fluff

The storefront and the brand system are built around a sense of craft and local reliability , which reinforces the credibility and character of the business.

The local campaign

A campaign built on proximity, speed and plain talk

With a direct, approachable tone that uses the language customers use we built a campaign that connects immediately with local buyers and reflects how the business actually talks. That local campaign continues across the store itself.

A MODERN STORE WITH CHARACTER

A clear, focused storefront

The online store of Pressform Print continues the local campaign consistently. With a clear, uncluttered structure, the focus stays firmly on the products and services on offer. At the same time, the authentic brand character carries through here too.
